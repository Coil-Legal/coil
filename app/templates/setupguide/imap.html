What this does

Watches a mailbox you forward or BCC to, and files those emails and their attachments onto the right matter automatically.

Do you need it? Optional, and a real time saver if you live in email. Without it you save documents to a matter by hand, which is what most people do at first.

Cost. Nothing. It reads a mailbox you already have.

Setting it up

  1. Pick which mailbox to watch. A dedicated address like files@yourfirm.com is tidier than your personal inbox.
  2. Create an app password for it, same as for sending.
  3. Gmail is imap.gmail.com port 993; Microsoft 365 is outlook.office365.com port 993.
  4. Then forward or BCC mail there with the matter number in brackets in the subject, like [M-1002]. Mail from a client with exactly one open matter files itself with no number needed.
