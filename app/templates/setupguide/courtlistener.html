What this does

Free case law search from the Free Law Project, with full opinion text and a citation checker that flags cites in a brief that do not resolve.

Do you need it? Optional, and search already works without a token. Adding one unlocks full opinion text, the citation check, and a higher rate limit.

Cost. Free. There is no paid tier to fall into.

Worth knowing. This checks whether a citation resolves, not whether a case is still good law. It is not a citator and will not replace Shepard's or KeyCite.

Getting the token

  1. Create a free account at courtlistener.com.
  2. Open your profile and copy the API token shown there.
  3. Use your own rather than borrowing one. Their terms forbid more than one account per organisation, and the free limit is 125 requests a day.
