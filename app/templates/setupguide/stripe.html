What this does

Lets clients pay an invoice online by card or bank transfer, from a link in the invoice email or in the client portal.

Do you need it? Only if you want to be paid online. Without it, invoices show your mailing and wire instructions instead and you record payments by hand as they arrive. Plenty of firms run that way for years.

Cost. No monthly fee. Stripe takes a percentage of each payment. Coil adds nothing on top and never touches the money: it goes straight to your Stripe account.

Getting the keys

  1. Create an account at stripe.com and finish their identity checks. Use the firm's legal name and bank details, not a personal account.
  2. In the dashboard open Developers, API keys and copy the secret and publishable keys.
  3. Still under Developers, open Webhooks, add the endpoint shown on the Integrations page, choose the event checkout.session.completed, and copy the signing secret.
  4. Start with test keys. A test key begins sk_test_ and a live one sk_live_, so you can try the whole flow before real money moves.
