What this does

Lets Coil send email on your behalf: invoices, engagement letters for signature, client portal invitations, payment reminders and your daily agenda.

Do you need it? In practice yes, sooner or later. Almost everything Coil sends a client goes through here. Without it Coil works normally and writes every message to a dev outbox you can read, but nothing reaches anyone.

Cost. Nothing. Use the mail account you already have.

Getting the details

  1. Create an app password in your email provider. Gmail and Microsoft 365 both require one for this. Your normal login password will not work, and you should not put it here.
  2. Your provider's SMTP server and port: Gmail is smtp.gmail.com port 587, Microsoft 365 is smtp.office365.com port 587.
  3. Send yourself a test invoice afterwards to confirm it arrives.
