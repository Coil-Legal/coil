New-matter workflows

Each enabled rule creates one ordinary task when a new matter matches all its conditions, including matters created through the API or import. Blank conditions match any matter. Due dates use calendar days after the matter's opening date. These are work reminders, not court deadlines.

Tasks go to the matter's responsible user, or stay unassigned. Rules do not run on existing matters, edits, or when the Tasks tool is off. Disabling or restoring rules does not remove tasks already created.
