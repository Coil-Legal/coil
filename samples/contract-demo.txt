FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT (DEMO)

This Agreement is made as of 1 March 2025 between Bluebonnet Logistics LLC, a Texas
limited liability company ("Shipper"), and Nordvale Freight Co. ("Carrier").

1. SERVICES. Carrier shall transport Shipper's goods between the points listed in
   Schedule A, on the schedule set out there.

2. RATES. Shipper shall pay the rates in Schedule B. Rates may be adjusted once per
   calendar year on sixty days' written notice.

3. TERM. This Agreement runs for twenty-four months from the date above and renews for
   successive twelve-month terms unless either party gives ninety days' written notice.

4. INSURANCE. Carrier shall maintain commercial auto liability coverage of not less than
   $1,000,000 per occurrence and shall name Shipper as an additional insured.

5. INDEMNITY. Carrier shall indemnify and hold Shipper harmless from claims arising out
   of Carrier's negligence in the performance of this Agreement, excluding claims arising
   from Shipper's own negligence.

6. DRIVER QUALIFICATION. Carrier warrants that every driver operating under this
   Agreement holds a current commercial licence and complies with all applicable
   hours-of-service regulations. Carrier shall retain records of compliance for the
   period required by law and produce them to Shipper on reasonable request.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Neither party is liable for consequential damages, except
   that this clause does not limit either party's indemnity obligations under Section 5.

8. GOVERNING LAW. Texas law governs, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

9. DISPUTES. The parties shall attempt mediation in good faith before filing suit.
